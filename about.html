How I think about design

Start with reality

Understand what users actually do before deciding what they should do.

Ship learning loops

Prototype, test, iterate—design as an evolving system, not a final product.

Empathy is structured

Observe behavior, ask good questions, and validate with data.

Design within constraints

Technical feasibility is part of the creative process—code your prototypes to know where the edges are.
